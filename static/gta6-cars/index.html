What this does

  • Adds cars copied from real saves, looking exactly as they did there (paint, wheels and so on).
  • Uses one of your empty garage slots (one per garage you own). In game, added cars first show up in your main garage (usually Caledonia Boulevard). Drive one to another garage you own and save, and it stays there.
  • Can replace the car in one of your slots.
  • Can add a car parked outside, under My Vehicles.

What this does NOT do

  • Change cars you already own (paint, upgrades, plates, tuning).
  • Give you garages or properties, or more slots than garages you own.
  • Add boats, planes, helicopters or military vehicles. (Cars it hasn't seen yet can be added from your own save; see below.)
  • Give you two of the same car, or put two cars in one slot.
